*Ud i det fri* means "out in the open". The app helps you plan a trip outdoors:
a short wizard suggests destinations, and from there you get a trip plan, a
meal plan with recipes, and a shopping list.

The part I care about most is the **shared packing list**. The group can see
who brings the tent and who brings the stove, so nobody packs three of
everything.

## Where it is now

A clickable prototype running on real OpenStreetMap maps. There is no backend
yet, and all the data is mock data.
