Ownform turns bodyweight training into a plan you can actually follow. Tell it
where you are today and what you are working towards, and it lays out the
weeks ahead: phased progression, a varied mix of accessory work, and
sessions drawn from a library of 66 exercises.

It runs on my own phone every week. For a training app, that is the only test
that counts.

## Where it is now

The plan generator has been through three rounds of rework, and the exercise
library keeps growing.
